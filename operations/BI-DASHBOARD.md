# Client intelligence dashboard

Production v1.19.1: Rastreamento → client → Dashboard. The executive view uses the existing first-observed-contact cohort, keeps Linktree under Instagram and preserves unknown origins. The default period is the last 30 Brasília calendar days. Channel cards open their acquisition details. Unit filtering uses explicit unit/unidade/utm_term values only, never message text guesses. Commercial summaries apply the same unit filter.

Meta Insights are queried on demand from the server, using the encrypted credential, fixed Graph host, bearer header and cursor-only bounded pagination. Account-level totals provide reach and frequency; they are not summed across days or campaigns. Daily campaign results, period campaign aggregates and period ad results are distinct. Spend, CPC/CPM and all ad-metric responses require the existing can_view_financials profile flag in the server. No ad configuration, budget or creative is modified. No Meta conversions are labelled confirmed sales.

CPL is withheld when the selected period begins before the first observed Orbe history. Even for a covered period, the label is **Custo por lead observado**, based on first contacts identified as Meta, not a guaranteed complete measure of platform-attributed leads. The user sees the coverage warning. No fake zeros for unconfigured providers.

Google remains OAuth pending. Site/GA4 read reporting and Instagram organic insights remain not connected; their observed WhatsApp leads and campaign origins are available. GA4 Measurement Protocol secrets used to emit events do not grant GA4 reporting access. EVO/ABC revenue, enrollments, CAC and LTV are not implemented.

Lead goals: wa_bi_goals stores positive monthly lead targets per connection and unit. Existing client visibility controls read access; active admins with visibility can write. Anonymous access and financial tables are untouched. No target values or enrollments were invented. Goals use whole-month first-contact cohorts, regardless of the origin filter; users can select the goals month. Projections are linear lead projections only.
